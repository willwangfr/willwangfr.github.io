Lenia

A continuous cellular automaton. Conway's Game of Life lives on a discrete on/off grid; Lenia replaces it with a smooth field, a ring-shaped kernel, and a smooth growth function — and out of that continuity swim lifelike creatures. The default is Orbium, a self-stabilizing glider.
